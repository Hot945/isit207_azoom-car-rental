THE STORY BEHIND AZOOM

Pioneering Singapore’s Clean Mobility Revolution

AZoom was born from a singular conviction: renting a car in Singapore should be whisper-quiet, entirely effortless, and 100% tailpipe-emission-free. Frustrated by outdated paper contracts, confusing fuel return policies, and noisy engines, we set out to build the island's first all-electric, zero-carbon rental experience.

Tailpipe Exhaust. Noise Pollution.

Singapore’s urban density demands a smarter, cleaner approach to road transportation. Internal combustion engines produce toxic tailpipe pollutants and mechanical racket that disrupt our living spaces.

At AZoom, every vehicle we deploy is powered purely by electrons. By partnering with leading charging networks across the island, we ensure seamless energy access from Changi to Tuas, allowing you to travel sustainably without range anxiety.
